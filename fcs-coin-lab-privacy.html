Privacy Policy for FindCoin

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

R's Fun Coin Shop ("we," "our," or "us") operates the FindCoin mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use FindCoin.

1. Information We Collect

  • Coin Photographs & Camera Access: When you use FindCoin to scan a coin, the App requests access to your device's camera or photo gallery. We collect the front and back images of the coins you capture or upload solely to identify the coin, estimate its value, and display your scan results. Coin photos you scan are also processed by automated image analysis to produce your identification and value estimate (see Section 3).
  • Subscription Payments: If you subscribe to unlimited scans, your payment is processed by the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store) through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never see or store your card details.
  • Expert Verdict Payments: The one-time Captain Heri expert verdict ($9.99) is processed securely through Stripe. We do not collect, view, or store your full credit card or bank account numbers on our servers. We only receive confirmation of payment status, transaction IDs, and the email address or order details necessary to fulfill your request.
  • Email Address: If you submit a coin for a Captain Heri expert verdict, we collect the email address you enter in the App so we can deliver your verdict and communicate with you about your submission. FindCoin does not require or offer user accounts — there is no sign-up.
  • App Usage Data: Your free-scan allowance is counted on your device only and is not transmitted to us. We may collect basic, non-identifying technical diagnostics (such as crash logs) strictly to keep the App fast and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Analyze coin images and provide automated identification and market value estimates.
  • Fulfill optional paid numismatic review requests ("Verify with Captain Heri") through R's Fun Coin Shop.
  • Manage free scan limits, subscriptions, and customer support inquiries.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the accuracy and performance of the App.

We never sell, rent, or trade your email address or uploaded coin photographs to third parties or advertisers.

3. Third-Party Services & Cloud Storage

We partner with trusted third-party providers to operate FindCoin securely. All data transmitted between the App and these services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols:

  • Supabase: Used for secure cloud database management and storing uploaded coin scan images so the App can process and display your results.
  • Google (Gemini API): Coin images are transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service, which performs the automated identification and analysis behind your scan results. While our Gemini API access remains on Google's unpaid tier, Google's terms permit Google to use submitted content to provide, improve, and develop Google products, and human reviewers may access API inputs and outputs. Please do not include personal information in your coin photos.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscription status and entitlements on our behalf, using your app-store account; no payment card data passes through RevenueCat to us.
  • Stripe: Processes only the one-time payment for the Captain Heri expert verdict (a human review service fulfilled outside the App by email), in compliance with PCI-DSS security standards.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or coin images to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Data Deletion

We retain your coin scan images and transaction records only as long as necessary to provide the App's services, fulfill your verification orders, and comply with legal or tax obligations.

How to Request Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of your scan history or uploaded coin photos at any time by emailing us at Funcoins2shop@gmail.com with the subject line "FindCoin Data Deletion Request," or by using our data deletion page. We will process and confirm your deletion request within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

FindCoin is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

6. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures — including encrypted network transmission and restricted database access controls — to protect your uploaded images and order data against unauthorized access or loss.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
