Privacy Policy for FCS Coin Lab

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

FCS Coin Lab ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the FCS Coin Lab: Value & Errors mobile application ("the App"), how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

You can use the App's coin specification, metal composition, and error watchlist lookup features without creating an account or providing personal information. We only collect personal data if you voluntarily request a paid personal expert verdict:

  • Email Address: Collected solely so we can deliver your personal numismatic review and communicate with you regarding your submission.
  • Coin Photographs: Front, back, or edge photos of your coin that you upload for evaluation.
  • Payment Confirmation Details: Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not receive, store, or have access to your full credit card or bank account numbers. We only store a Stripe transaction reference ID to confirm payment.
  • Anonymous App Diagnostics: Basic, non-identifying scan logs and crash diagnostics used strictly to keep the App fast and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively to:

  • Perform your requested 1-on-1 numismatic evaluation and email your personal verdict to you.
  • Verify payment and issue refunds if needed.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access to uploaded submissions.

We never sell, rent, or trade your email address or uploaded coin photographs to third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your uploaded coin photos and submission details are stored in access-restricted, encrypted cloud storage (Supabase) accessible only to authorized FCS Coin Lab reviewers. You may request deletion of your email and submitted photos at any time by emailing Funcoins2shop@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

FCS Coin Lab is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to delete your submission data, contact us at: Funcoins2shop@gmail.com.
