FindCoin — Data Deletion Request

FindCoin, operated by R's Fun Coin Shop, does not use user accounts. You can still request deletion of any data associated with you at any time.

What you can ask us to delete

  • Coin photographs you uploaded for scanning or for a Captain Heri expert verdict.
  • The email address you provided for verdict delivery, and the associated submission record.
  • Scan submission records stored in our cloud database.

Your free-scan count is stored only on your own device and is removed automatically when you uninstall the App — we cannot see or delete it for you. Transaction records that we are legally required to keep for tax and accounting purposes (for example, payment confirmations from Stripe or your app store) may be retained for the period the law requires, with all other associated data deleted.

How to request deletion

Email Funcoins2shop@gmail.com with the subject line "FindCoin Data Deletion Request." Please send the request from, or mention, the email address you used for any Captain Heri verdict submission so we can locate your records.

We will process and confirm your deletion request within 30 days.
